# Complexity != Difficulty
<div class="columns">
<div>

## Difficulty (COMPLICATED)
- Amount of time and effort
- Circumstances and conditions
- Confidence and capability of the practitioner
- Accuracy of execution
- Percentage of practitioners capable of executing correctly

</div>
<div>

## Complexity
- Type of thinking
- Intricacy of knowledge
- Specificity of expectations
- Scope of activity
- Abstractness of concepts
- Quality of responses

</div>
</div>

<!--
** Complexity is somewhat counter intuitive.  Complexity != Risk
** Difficult and Complex are not the same thing.
** A requirement for greater cognitive ability is accompanied by a higher risk tolerance.
-->

# Radius = MPH
<div style='margin-top:70px'></div>
<div class="columns">
<div>

<b>The Art</b>
- The track is the framework, it’s up to the rider maximize velocity.
- Find your line, different based on traffic, track conditions, etc..
- Brake, tip in, hit the apex, point and straighten the bike, accelerate, repeat
- Eat what you cook (At high velocity we cannot afford to harden the wrong neuro pathways)
- Be smooth
